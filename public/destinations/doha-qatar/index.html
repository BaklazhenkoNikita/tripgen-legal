Doha Travel Guide

Doha is a dazzling, rapidly evolving capital city that seamlessly blends futuristic skyscrapers with deep-rooted Arabian traditions. Situated on the coast of the Persian Gulf, it serves as the vibrant heart of Qatar, offering a unique mix of high-end luxury, world-class museums, and historic charm.

About Doha

Founded in the 1820s as a modest fishing and pearling village, Doha grew from the older settlement of Al Bidda. For much of the early 20th century, the city's economy was almost entirely dependent on the pearl trade, which collapsed in the 1930s, leading to a period of economic hardship until the discovery of oil in 1939 transformed the nation's fortunes. Following Qatar's independence from British protection in 1971, Doha was officially designated the capital. In recent decades, the city has undergone a breathtaking transformation, fueled by vast natural gas reserves, to become a global hub for commerce, education, and international sporting events, most notably hosting the 2022 FIFA World Cup.

Doha's culture is a fascinating tapestry where ancient Bedouin heritage meets a cosmopolitan, modern lifestyle. Traditional customs, such as the importance of the _majlis_ (a communal gathering space) and the practice of falconry, remain central to Qatari identity, even as the city embraces cutting-edge architecture and global trends. The food scene is a vibrant reflection of this diversity, offering everything from authentic Qatari dishes like _machboos_ (spiced rice with meat) to high-end international fine dining. The arts are also a major focus, with the city investing heavily in world-class institutions that bridge the gap between Islamic history and contemporary global art.

Quick facts

  • The name 'Doha' is derived from the Arabic word 'ad-Dawha,' meaning 'the roundness,' a reference to the circular shape of the bay upon which the city was built.
  • The National Museum of Qatar is architecturally designed to resemble a 'desert rose' crystal, a natural formation found in the Qatari desert.
  • Doha is one of the most rapidly urbanized cities in the world, with significant portions of its modern landscape built on reclaimed land.
  • The city is home to a massive expatriate population, with over 100 nationalities living and working in the metropolitan area.
  • Qatar has one of the world's most skewed gender ratios, with a significantly higher percentage of men than women due to the large migrant labor force.
