Naples Travel Guide

Naples is a vibrant, chaotic, and soul-stirring coastal city that serves as the historic heart of southern Italy. It is a place where ancient Greek foundations, opulent royal palaces, and a legendary culinary scene collide against the dramatic backdrop of Mount Vesuvius.

About Naples

Founded by Greek settlers in the 8th century BC as Parthenope and later re-established as Neapolis (meaning 'new city'), Naples is one of the oldest continuously inhabited urban areas in the world. Throughout its long history, it has been a crucial Mediterranean hub, flourishing under Roman rule and later serving as the capital of the Kingdom of Naples and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies for centuries. This rich past has left the city with an incredible density of historical layers, from ancient catacombs and Roman ruins to medieval fortresses and grand Baroque architecture. It remained a powerful independent capital until the unification of Italy in 1861, a transition that fundamentally shifted its political and economic landscape.

Neapolitan culture is famously intense, passionate, and deeply rooted in tradition. The city is the undisputed birthplace of **pizza**, and locals take their food—from wood-fired Margherita to fresh seafood and sfogliatella pastries—with religious seriousness. The streets are a living theater, characterized by bustling markets, narrow alleys like Spaccanapoli, and a resilient, expressive local spirit that defines the city's unique identity. Art and music are woven into the fabric of daily life, with the city boasting world-class museums and a long history of operatic excellence. Visitors will find a city that is _unapologetically authentic_, where the noise and energy of the streets are matched only by the warmth and hospitality of its people.

Quick facts

  • The city's name, 'Napoli', comes from the Greek word 'Neapolis', meaning 'new city'.
  • Legend claims that the Castel dell'Ovo ('Egg Castle') is named after a magical egg hidden in its foundation by the poet Virgil; if the egg breaks, the castle and the city will fall.
  • Naples has one of the largest historic centers in the world, which is a designated UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • The city is home to three major castles: Castel Nuovo, Castel dell'Ovo, and Castel Sant'Elmo.
  • Naples is the birthplace of the modern pizza, specifically the Margherita, created to honor Queen Margherita of Savoy.
